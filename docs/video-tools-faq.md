
# Online Video Tools: Frequently Asked Questions

## What are online video tools?

Online video tools are browser-based services that help users work with digital video content without necessarily installing desktop software.

## What is video resolution?

Video resolution describes the number of pixels in a video image. Common resolutions include 480p, 720p, 1080p, and 4K.

## Which video format should I use?

MP4 is widely supported across devices and platforms. WebM is another common format for web-based video. The best choice depends on your intended use and device compatibility.

## Is downloading every online video permitted?

No. Permission depends on copyright law, the rights holder, and the applicable platform terms. Always ensure you have the necessary rights before downloading or saving content.
